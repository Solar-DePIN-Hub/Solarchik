Solarchik roof run - audio credits and licence
==============================================

All roof-run music and sound effects are ORIGINAL works, synthesised from code
(numpy additive/subtractive synthesis, synthetic drums and noise, a synthetic
reverb impulse) by the scripts in android/tools/run-audio/ (synth.py, music.py,
sfx.py). No samples, loops, presets or recordings from any third party were
used, and no existing melody was transcribed.

Regenerate: python3 tools/run-audio/music.py OUT && python3 tools/run-audio/sfx.py OUT/sfx

The previous web theme (assets/audio/theme.ogg) is no longer shipped.
